# lab/ - your workspace
- `answers/N-rootcause.env`, `answers/N-evidence.env`, `postmortem-N.md`: yours for incident N (templates are shipped).
- `incident-N/`: generated by `./prepare-lab.sh N`: your files (see the page) and GIVEN helpers (`restart.sh`, `apply.sh`, `apply-keys.sh`, `rollout.sh`). Re-running prepare gives a new variant and backs the old folder up to `lab.bak-*`.
