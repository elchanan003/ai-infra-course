# 11 — Lab: arrive with a plan

Time: ~20 min. Type: Reflect -> Plan. No services needed.

## Situation

Your onboarding starts soon. The team will assume you know the concepts; what they cannot give you is a clear
picture of your own gaps and a list of good questions. Turn the course into three short documents you will
actually use in week 1.

## Ownership

| File | Status |
|---|---|
| `lab/self-assessment.md`, `lab/team-questions.md`, `lab/first-week-plan.md` | yours - fill in |
| scripts | given - do not modify |

Commands (from `modules/11-onboarding-prep/`): `./prepare-lab.sh`, `./verify.sh`. `verify.sh` only checks that the files
are genuinely filled in; the tutor reviews content with you.

## Task 1 - Self-assessment (8 min)

Rate every skill 1-5 honestly (compare with PROGRESS.md weak areas and verify scores). The file lists 14 skills
across the four layers, artifacts/air-gap, gateway governance, observability, GPU on Kubernetes and incident work.
For each skill rated 1-3 write a concrete next step (what you will practise or ask, by when).

**Success criteria**
- 11.1 all 14 skills rated 1-5; every skill rated 1-3 has a concrete next step

## Task 2 - Questions for the team (7 min)

Using theory section 2 and your "Open questions for the team" in PROGRESS.md, write at least 10 questions you will
really ask, across at least 4 sections. Order them: the ones that change your first week first.

**Success criteria**
- 11.2 at least 10 distinct questions in at least 4 sections

## Task 3 - First-week plan (5 min)

Adapt the template in theory section 5 to what you know about the team: for each day the focus and a concrete output.

**Success criteria**
- 11.3 days 1-5 each have a focus and an output

## Done

`./verify.sh` passes. Then go through the three files with the tutor, and copy them somewhere you will have at work.
